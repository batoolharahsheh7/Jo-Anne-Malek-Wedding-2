OUR STORY

Our story began with a trip to Jordan in 2019. While Jordan was already home to one of us, that first trip sparked a connection that continued long after the journey ended. Once back home, we started talking and, over the years, always found our way back to each other, despite the distance and the different countries we called home.

For years, our connection lived through conversations and across borders. Then, in 2025, an unexpected trip to Luxembourg finally made it real — and for the first time, we were together in person.

After all these years, we’re finally able to share our lives without the distance between us.

And now, we only hope that no one has to catch a flight just to see the person they love — except this time, we hope you’ll catch one to Jordan to celebrate our love with us.
